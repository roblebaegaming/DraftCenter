## Trend and interpretation

Traffic rose sharply beginning August 11, peaked at 1,128 daily visitors on
August 14, and recorded 581 to 695 daily visitors from August 18 through the
partial August 20 snapshot.

The previous August 1 baseline recorded 51 visitors and 206 page views during
the first short collection period. The current counts are roughly 160 times
and 102 times those totals, respectively, but this is not an apples-to-apples
growth rate because the reporting windows differ.

The site now has enough observed traffic to proceed with AdSense preparation
and site review once privacy, consent, audience, and intellectual-property
gates are satisfied. The proposed first ad inventory remains selected original
English guide articles. Because no guide appears in the current top five, the
eligible guide page-view baseline must be measured separately before deciding
whether to activate the experiment.
