# DraftCenter SEO, content, and outside-reference roadmap

**Period:** August 3–December 31, 2026
**Production:** https://www.draftcentral.gg
**Primary measurement:** Google Search Console
**Supporting measurement:** Vercel Web Analytics

## Starting point

Search Console on August 3 reported:

- 43 impressions;
- 1 click;
- 2.3% click-through rate;
- average position 26.7;
- one accepted sitemap with 1,059 discovered URLs; and
- indexing and experience reports still processing.

Early queries include `gengar stats`, `vgc draft`, `what is the current pokemon
vgc format`, and `pokemon draft league resources`. This is too little data for
firm conclusions, but it supports three initial content clusters:

1. Pokémon draft profiles and statistics;
2. current and historical VGC/draft formats; and
3. practical draft-league education for commissioners and coaches.

## Principles

- Help people complete a real draft-league task; do not publish filler to meet a quota.
- Improve existing pages before creating near-duplicates.
- Use DraftCenter's first-party community data when the sample is large enough and show sample size and methodology.
- Keep Pokémon names, formats, and legality facts accurate and date-sensitive.
- Add internal links where they genuinely help the reader continue a task.
- Seek references by giving communities useful tools, data, or guides—not by buying links, mass-submitting directories, or trading links.
- Keep outreach personal and low volume. Never scrape or bulk-message community members.
- Do not expose private league, account, Discord, or uploaded team data.

## Measurement cadence

Every Monday, record a seven-day Search Console snapshot:

- total clicks and impressions;
- average CTR and position;
- indexed and non-indexed page counts;
- top 10 queries and pages;
- queries with positions 8–30;
- pages with high impressions but below-site-average CTR; and
- crawl/indexing reasons that affect more than 10 URLs or a key landing page.

On the first Monday of each month, compare the previous 28 days with the prior
28 days. Annotate releases, content publication, community posts, and unusual
events so changes are interpretable.

Do not react to daily ranking movement. Review a new or substantially revised
page after 14 days, and make a decision after 28 days unless Search Console
shows a technical error.

## October: publish distinctive first-party data

Publishing target: one data study, one methodology page, and one supporting guide.

Recommended study:

- **DraftCenter Pokémon Draft Trends: Most Drafted, Biggest Risers, and Best Value Picks**

Requirements:

- show the date range, eligible formats, completed-draft rules, exclusions, and sample sizes;
- distinguish popularity, ADP, auction price, and win rate;
- avoid declaring causation from correlation;
- suppress or qualify tiny samples; and
- link every highlighted Pokémon to its profile.

Supporting content:

- **How to Use ADP Without Copying the Room**
- a permanent **DraftCenter Data Methodology** page that future studies can cite.

Outside-reference work:

- Give participating leagues an embeddable summary card or concise findings they can share.
- Pitch the study individually to a small number of Pokémon draft community newsletters, podcasts, creators, or resource curators whose audiences match it.
- Offer the anonymized aggregate table as a downloadable CSV if privacy and sample-size checks pass.
- Publish one transparent methodology note alongside every data release.

October success check:

- the study earns qualified visits or references from at least two relevant community sources;
- profile pages receive internal traffic from the study; and
- Search Console begins showing queries beyond exact Pokémon-stat lookups.

- list the pages and queries responsible for at least 80% of organic impressions;

- choose the three strongest clusters for Q1 2027; and
- set 2027 targets from the observed baseline rather than an arbitrary traffic promise.

## Decision thresholds

- **Indexing:** investigate immediately if an important page is blocked, marked `noindex`, canonicalized elsewhere unexpectedly, or fails retrieval. Otherwise allow 14–28 days for new pages.
- **Sitemap:** investigate if submitted URLs fall unexpectedly, fetching fails, or key page types disappear. A discovered-page count alone is not an indexed-page count.
- **CTR:** consider a snippet revision when a page has at least 100 impressions and underperforms pages at a similar position.
- **Near-page-one queries:** prioritize relevant queries in positions 8–20 with repeat impressions; strengthen the matching page and internal links.
- **Thin profile risk:** if many Pokémon pages remain crawled but unindexed after 6–8 weeks, improve only profiles with demand/data and reconsider whether every profile belongs in the sitemap.
- **Content quality:** consolidate or stop a series if several entries receive no impressions, referrals, or meaningful product use after two review cycles.
- **Outside references:** value relevant editorial references and qualified referral visits over raw backlink counts.

## Sustainable operating rhythm

Each month:

- publish two strong editorial assets, except October and December flagship work;
- refresh one proven page based on query data;
- improve 5–10 internal links;
- conduct no more than five personalized outreach conversations;
- record Search Console and referral outcomes; and
- reserve one week for product reliability and community work rather than forcing publication.

This cadence is intentionally modest. DraftCenter should build a small library
of definitive, maintained resources and distinctive community data—not a large
archive of generic articles.
