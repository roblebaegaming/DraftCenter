## Message principles

- Lead with the resource and the problem it solves, not search rankings.
- Personalize every message and keep it to one relevant recipient or community.
- Ask for corrections or feedback before asking for attention.
- Do not buy, trade, or require links.
- Do not scrape members, send bulk direct messages, or repeat unanswered asks.
- Credit contributors only with their permission.
- Record the date, destination, resource, response, resulting reference, and
  referral visits so useful relationships matter more than backlink counts.
