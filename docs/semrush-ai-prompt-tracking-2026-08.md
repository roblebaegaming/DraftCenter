## What to record

For each weekly review, record:

- DraftCenter mentions by platform;
- DraftCenter citations and cited pages;
- prompts with a new, lost, or changed mention;
- competitor mentions for the same prompt;
- cited external sources that repeatedly influence the answer; and
- whether the matching DraftCenter page actually answers the prompt well.

Do not rewrite a page after one volatile result. Improve a page when the same
gap repeats, the page is the correct match for the prompt, and the change makes
the resource more useful to a human reader.
