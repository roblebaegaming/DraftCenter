# DraftCenter campaign link standard

Date: August 20, 2026 Pacific

Use one destination, one stable campaign, and one language/creative identifier
for every placement. The application validates the same four-field contract:

`utm_source` + `utm_medium` + `utm_campaign` + `utm_content`

## Naming

- Sources: `discord`, `reddit`, `x`, `instagram`, `facebook`, `bluesky`,
  `youtube`, `email`, or a reviewed partner slug.
- Media: `community`, `social`, `paid-social`, `paid-search`, `video`, `email`,
  or `referral`.
- Campaigns stay stable for the complete push, such as `worlds-2026`,
  `team-lab-battle-room`, or `run-a-complete-league`.
- Content is `language-creative-variant`, such as `en-overview-1`,
  `it-odds-1`, or `es-player-picks-2`.

Never put a person, email, username, account, league, team, opponent, Pokémon,
private note, or other personal detail in a UTM value.

## Review cadence

Record the publication date and review aggregate account creations, signup
starts, and relevant product journeys after 3, 7, and 30 days. Compare a
three-day average before changing spend. Do not infer a campaign outcome from
an untagged placement or one day of traffic.
