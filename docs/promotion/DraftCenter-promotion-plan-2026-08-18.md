# DraftCenter promotion plan

Prepared August 17, 2026 Pacific. This replaces the dated August 15 promotion
pack for new posts while preserving the older Instagram folders as historical
released-site assets.

## Launch order

1. **Team Lab and Battle Room:** lead with the one-tap ladder workflow, fast
   result controls, private turn recorder, performance analytics, replay and
   rating history, and the new 10-sheet workbook.
2. **Auction Draft Tournaments:** show a complete hosted auction feeding Swiss
   or elimination play for 4–32 managers.
3. **Prediction tournaments:** introduce the public upcoming/live/past hub and
   the reusable official-bracket system.
4. **Organizations:** promote the open organization directory and private,
   opt-out-aware commissioner/member email workflow.
5. **Legends and Alpha Dex:** show the Legends: Z-A Pokédex and optional Alpha
   checklists for Legends: Arceus and Legends: Z-A.
6. **Pokémon research:** use the five improved Pokémon profiles and the type,
   generation, form, and comparison paths as evergreen search content.

Do not combine all six topics in one post. Use one feature, one destination,
and one stable campaign name per placement.

## Supporting campaign messages

### Auction Draft Tournaments

Hook: **One auction room, one authoritative clock, and a complete 4–32 manager
tournament path.** Show bot automation only in a practice event, keep the
human nomination and bidding windows visible, and show the handoff into Swiss
or elimination after rosters lock.

### Prediction tournaments

Hook: **Upcoming, live, and past official prediction brackets now share one
permanent home.** Do not publish guessed fields, unpublished event URLs, or
private entries. The owner publisher remains an operations workflow, not a
public submission tool.

### Organizations

Hook: **Join open DraftCenter communities without asking a commissioner to add
you manually.** Email claims must say recipients remain private and can opt
out. Do not expose or demonstrate real recipient addresses.

### Legends and Alpha Dex

Hook: **Track the Legends Pokédex you actually have, including an optional
Alpha checklist that excludes Alpha-locked species.** Do not claim that every
individual can be Alpha or that DraftCenter has released Z-A encounter
locations; those locations remain unavailable.

## Publishing and measurement

- Use one channel-specific link per placement and keep the campaign name
  stable for the full push.
- Record the actual publication date; review aggregate Operations results after
  3, 7, and 30 days.
- Prioritize attributed account creations, then signup starts and relevant
  feature journeys. Do not inspect or publish individual identities.
- Treat the older Instagram sets under `docs/promotion/instagram/` as historical
  snapshots. Do not reuse an older frame when current behavior is materially
  different.
- Get owner approval for the exact external audience before sending partner,
  tester, member-email, or beta invitations.
