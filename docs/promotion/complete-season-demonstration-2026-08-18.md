# Complete-season demonstration plan

This proof package uses a fictional league. It must never be presented as a
customer case study, lighthouse outcome, or Production adoption result.

## Synthetic league

- League: **Indigo Circuit**
- Season: **Founders Season**
- Format: six-team first-season preset
- Draft: snake, eight Pokémon per roster
- Schedule: round robin, top-four playoff
- Managers and teams: invented names with no connection to real accounts
- Replays: public test-safe examples or locally mocked official payloads; never
  a private or password-protected replay

Use a disposable private practice league for capture. Verify its exact league
identifier before any cleanup. Do not use Mushroom Cup, the intentionally
paused Mushroom Hut drafts, or another real league.

## The 75-second story

| Time | Product proof | Commissioner message |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–7 seconds | Public home and **Run a league** | “Run the whole league in one place.” |
| 7–16 seconds | Private practice path and first-season preset | “Start with a safe rehearsal or a recommended real-season setup.” |
| 16–27 seconds | Spreadsheet preview with teams, prices, and exact forms | “Move an existing league without a partial or silent import.” |
| 27–36 seconds | Invite link, claimed teams, launch checklist, scheduled draft | “Everyone sees one shared source of truth.” |
| 36–46 seconds | Completed draft board and generated schedule | “The draft becomes the season without rebuilding data.” |
| 46–57 seconds | Showdown replay analysis and explicit player mapping | “Confirm official replay facts; never guess hidden stats.” |
| 57–66 seconds | Result, standings, and weekly next action | “Managers and commissioners always know what happens next.” |
| 66–75 seconds | Playoff champion, frozen season archive, export | “Finish with a season record you can preserve and run again.” |

## Required captures

Capture desktop and phone-width evidence for:

1. the public commissioner promise and three primary paths;
2. the signed-in weekly next-action panel;
3. preset selection and the five-step launch checklist;
4. a blocked import with a downloadable error report;
5. a valid preview before confirmation;
6. a complete roster import warning and exact-name confirmation;
7. Showdown player mapping, supported facts, and the no-KO/no-unrevealed limit;
8. the confirmed replay badge after the normal result save;
9. standings, playoffs, champion, and frozen season history; and
10. the aggregate-only commissioner activation panel in owner Operations.

Every capture must use synthetic identities and data. Crop provider secrets,
browser session material, emails, account IDs, league UUIDs, private messages,
and unrelated tabs.

## Proof checklist

Before publishing, verify that the demonstration:

- uses the exact current labels and released behavior;
- does not imply battle-client integration, automatic knockout credit, or
  knowledge of unrevealed brought Pokémon;
- does not imply manager labels create accounts or claims;
- distinguishes a preview from a saved import;
- shows a completed season rather than a collection of unrelated features;
- includes one destination and one stable campaign name;
- has explicit owner approval for the final channel and audience; and
- is re-recorded if the product path changes materially.

Real commissioner proof is a later artifact. It requires a completed or
meaningfully active lighthouse season plus separate permission for every quote,
logo, league identity, screenshot, and outcome used.
