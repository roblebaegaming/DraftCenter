# DraftCenter Collector Founding Beta

This is the owner-run recruitment and feedback workflow for a first cohort of
five to ten collectors. It is preparation only. Do not contact people, publish
an invitation, collect identities, or choose a community destination without
the owner's explicit audience approval.

## Cohort shape

Aim for eight people if possible:

- two living-dex or HOME organizers;
- two shiny hunters;
- one collector with multiple individual Pokémon of the same species;
- one Pokémon Bank user with legacy records to organize;
- one spreadsheet-first collector; and
- one collector new to tracking tools.

Participant records belong in the owner's approved private system, not this
repository. Use anonymous slot labels in product notes.

## Invitation draft

> I’m testing DraftCenter Collector, a free private Pokédex and individual-
> collection organizer. Would you be willing to spend about 20–30 minutes on a
> real collection task and send candid feedback? You can use a disposable test
> tracker and omit any private notes. The test includes CSV import, JSON backup,
> and an Excel/Google Sheets-ready workbook. This is product research, not a
> sales call, and contributing money is never required.

Before sending, the owner must choose the recipients, channel, timing, and any
incentive. Do not place this message in editorial Discord channels, league
channels, or direct messages interchangeably; each is a separate scope.

## Twenty-to-thirty-minute task

1. Create a game or HOME tracker and mark at least five entries.
2. Add an optional ball, ribbon, or private note to one entry.
3. Add one storage location and two individual Pokémon, including a duplicate
   species if that matches the collection.
4. Download the CSV template, import at least one checklist row and one
   individual row, and explain anything unclear before confirming.
5. Download the open-tracker JSON, restore it, and verify that a new copy was
   created without changing the original.
6. Download the Collector workbook and open it in Excel or import it into
   Google Sheets.
7. If relevant, review the Bank/HOME source date and explain what the warning
   does and does not claim.
8. On a phone, try the focused install flow or read the manual instructions.

Use disposable or deliberately non-sensitive notes during observation. Never
ask for Nintendo credentials, save files, payment details, an account password,
or recovery material.

## Feedback checklist

1. Which game or HOME collection did you try?
2. Did creating or restoring a tracker feel clear?
3. If you imported a CSV, what was confusing or slow?
4. Could you find and edit an individual Pokémon quickly?
5. Did the workbook contain the information you expected?
6. Was any Bank or HOME guidance unclear or too confident?
7. What one convenience feature would make this worth supporting?
8. Would you prefer a one-time contribution or an optional subscription for
   future convenience features?
9. Anything you expected DraftCenter Collector to do that it did not?

The in-product “Copy tester checklist” button copies these questions without an
email address, webhook, or invented submission destination.

## Evidence and decision rule

Summarize feedback by anonymous slot and theme. Do not paste names, emails,
private collection notes, uploaded files, or exact tracker contents into the
repository. Compare only coarse conversion signals: tracker creation, inventory
opening, successful import/restore, export, install choice, support-link choice,
and checklist copy.

After at least five completed sessions, choose one outcome:

- continue the beta if core tasks succeed and collectors request refinements;
- pause promotion and fix repeated blockers before recruiting more people; or
- stop the offer if trust, privacy, or usefulness concerns outweigh demand.

Funding interest is supporting evidence, not the sole success criterion.
Current tools remain free regardless of the result.
