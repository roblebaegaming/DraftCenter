# DraftCenter Collector Founding Beta

This is the owner-run recruitment and feedback workflow for a first cohort of
five to ten collectors. It is preparation only. Do not contact people, publish
an invitation, collect identities, or choose a community destination without
the owner's explicit audience approval.

## Cohort shape

Aim for eight people if possible:

- two living-dex or HOME organizers;
- two shiny hunters;
- one collector with multiple individual Pokémon of the same species;
- one player completing a game with separate area or DLC Pokédexes;
- one spreadsheet-first collector; and
- one collector new to tracking tools.

Participant records belong in the owner's approved private system, not this
repository. Use anonymous slot labels in product notes.

## Invitation draft

> I’m testing Pokédex Tracker, a free private checklist and collection organizer.
> Would you be willing to spend about 20–30 minutes trying a real collection task
> and sharing honest feedback? You can use a disposable tracker and leave out any
> private notes. I’m especially interested in whether the game-specific numbers,
> DLC sections, Pokémon finder, box view, and spreadsheet tools make sense. This
> is product research, not a sales call, and contributing money is never required.

Before sending, the owner must choose the recipients, channel, timing, and any
incentive. Do not place this message in editorial Discord channels, league
channels, or direct messages interchangeably; each is a separate scope.

## Twenty-to-thirty-minute task

1. Create a game tracker and mark at least five Pokémon.
2. Check that the Pokémon are in the same order and use the same numbers as the
   in-game Pokédex.
3. For a game with DLC, switch between the base-game and DLC area dexes and mark
   at least one Pokémon in each.
4. Use **Find** to look up a missing Pokémon and explain whether the game and
   location results answer the question clearly.
5. Open **Boxes**, move between two boxes, and mark a Pokémon from a box slot.
6. Create or open a HOME National Dex tracker and confirm that Pokémon marked in
   the game tracker also count there.
7. Add an optional ball, ribbon, or private note to one entry and, if useful,
   add one storage location and two individual Pokémon.
8. Download the CSV template or Collector workbook and explain anything unclear.
9. On a phone, try the focused install flow or read the manual instructions.

Use disposable or deliberately non-sensitive notes during observation. Never
ask for Nintendo credentials, save files, payment details, an account password,
or recovery material.

## Feedback checklist

1. Which game or HOME collection did you try?
2. Did the game-specific numbering and order match what you expected?
3. Were the base-game and DLC area dexes easy to understand and switch between?
4. Did Find show a useful answer for where to get a missing Pokémon?
5. Did the box layout make the checklist easier to use in the game?
6. Did linked National Dex progress behave the way you expected?
7. Could you find and edit an individual Pokémon quickly?
8. If you imported a CSV or opened the workbook, what was confusing or missing?
9. What one convenience feature would make this worth supporting?
10. Would you prefer a one-time contribution or an optional subscription for
   future convenience features?
11. Anything you expected Pokédex Tracker to do that it did not?

The in-product “Copy tester checklist” button copies these questions without an
email address, webhook, or invented submission destination.

## Evidence and decision rule

Summarize feedback by anonymous slot and theme. Do not paste names, emails,
private collection notes, uploaded files, or exact tracker contents into the
repository. Compare only coarse conversion signals: tracker creation, area-dex
switching, finder use, box use, inventory opening, successful import, export,
install choice, support-link choice, and checklist copy.

After at least five completed sessions, choose one outcome:

- continue the beta if core tasks succeed and collectors request refinements;
- pause promotion and fix repeated blockers before recruiting more people; or
- stop the offer if trust, privacy, or usefulness concerns outweigh demand.

Funding interest is supporting evidence, not the sole success criterion.
Current tools remain free regardless of the result.
